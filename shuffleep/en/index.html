Shuffleep is an app designed for a quiet session before sleep. It starts with a short meditation, then moves into Word Shuffle, which reads random words aloud one at a time.

Shuffleep is free to download, with an optional monthly or annual Premium subscription. No account required. The app is not available for iPhone or iPad.

What is the Cognitive Shuffle?

applelanternwave

The Cognitive Shuffle Sleep Method is a bedtime technique: you picture unrelated words, one after another, such as an apple, a lantern, or a wave. In Shuffleep, the app picks the words, reads them slowly, and shows each one on screen.

Listen to random words read aloud, or watch them appear on screen one after another, as a way to step away from bedtime thoughts.

The Cognitive Shuffle was proposed by Luc Beaudoin of Simon Fraser University in Canada. Shuffleep is an independent app inspired by the method. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by Luc Beaudoin or any related organization.

How a session flows

  1. 1Meditation

    A short narrated meditation, such as a breathing guide, to get ready for bed.

  2. 2Guide Voice

    A brief spoken explanation of how the shuffle works, right before it begins.

  3. 3Word Shuffle

    Words from your theme, read aloud one at a time at a steady pace.

  4. A timer ends the session

    20 minutes by default, adjustable from 15 to 120 minutes. The sound fades out gently at the end.

You can use Meditation or Word Shuffle on its own, as long as one of them is on. Your session setup is saved for the nights that follow.

Themes and sound

Each theme pairs a word set with an ambient sound, a visual, and music, and you can swap any of them.

  • Rain Terrace
  • Starry Campfire

  • Ocean Lullaby
  • Forest Breeze
  • A Custom theme lets you put together your own.

More features

Ambient sound
Rain, campfire, insects, waves, and forest. Each one is built on the not-quite-regular, not-quite-random variation found in natural sounds (1/f fluctuation) and plays softly behind the voice.
Visuals
Slow-moving scenes of rain, a campfire, fireflies, waves, and leaves.
Music
Plays under the voice and ambient sound. Moonlight in the free version, plus three more tracks with Premium.
When it plays
Set ambient sound and music to play for the whole session, only during the meditation, or only during the shuffle.
Preview
Listen to the voice, ambient sound, and music together before you start.
Volume
An overall level plus separate levels for voice, ambient sound, and music.
Word interval and speed
Adjust the interval between words and the playback speed.
Background playback
Turn it on to keep the session playing with the screen off (it may stop if battery optimization is enabled for the app).

Questions

What can I use for free?

Download Shuffleep for free and use the five themes, the ambient sounds and visuals, the Quick Intro meditation, the Moonlight track, and Word Shuffle. The free version shows ads; ads don’t appear while a session is playing.

How do I cancel?

In the store app, tap your profile icon, go to Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, and cancel before the renewal date. Uninstalling the app or deleting its data does not cancel your subscription. After you cancel, Premium stays available until the end of the period you have paid for.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. See the Privacy Policy for how the app handles information.

Can I listen with the screen off?

Turn on background playback in the settings to keep the session playing with the screen off. It may stop if battery optimization is enabled for the app.

Which devices can I use?

Shuffleep is available in the United States, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, and Singapore. It is not available for iPhone or iPad. Check the system requirements to see if your device is supported.

Which languages are supported?

The interface is available in English and Japanese. Audio (meditation narration, the guide voice, and word readings) is available only in Japanese and American English (en-US). British, Australian, and other English accents are not offered. Meditation narration, the guide voice, and theme word readings follow the app’s language setting: Japanese when the app is in Japanese, American English when it is in English. Custom Word Sets may include words read in a different language.
