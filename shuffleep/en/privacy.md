## Article 6 Security and Retention

1. The Operator takes necessary and appropriate security measures for the personal data it handles, such as restricting access to personal data on the Operator’s side to what is necessary for its work, encrypting communications (HTTPS), managing accounts and credentials for external services, and reviewing the contracts and settings of service providers. Where data is handled in a foreign country, the Operator takes these measures with an understanding of that country’s personal-information protection system. Details of the measures, except those whose disclosure could interfere with security management, will be provided without undue delay upon request to the contact in Article 14. The Operator has no feature collecting or storing store passwords or full card numbers.
2. Information on the device is kept until the User deletes it or the app deletes records that are no longer needed. Entitlement caches in the delivery infrastructure expire after a short time but may be created again on subsequent use. Subscription, update, delivery and advertising records, and access and operational records kept by the Operator and providers, are retained according to each service’s settings, terms and applicable law. Records relating to consent to automatic renewal kept by the Operator for legal compliance are governed by paragraph 4.
3. Inquiry and rights-request records are kept as needed for handling and deleted when no longer required. Records may be retained to the extent needed for legal retention, disputes, fraud prevention or other legitimate requirements. Users may inquire about retention or deletion of specific records.
4. Records needed to verify consent to automatic renewal, and the order and transaction information needed to match that consent to a purchase, are retained for the period required by applicable law, including U.S. state law. This retention does not end because automatic renewal is turned off, the app is uninstalled, or the customer record in the subscription management service (RevenueCat) is deleted. Other purchase-action records are retained as long as needed for purchase processing, matching and related purposes. In either case, records that no longer need to be kept are deleted. Purchase-action records on the device are kept as long as needed to confirm transmission and allow resending, and are deleted the next time the app processes them after that period. If a purchase is canceled, the app deletes records not yet sent, but records already passed to the SDK may still be sent.

## Article 9 Children and Minors

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and persons under 13 may not use it. If the Operator learns that personal information of a child under 13 has been collected, it will stop relevant processing, make necessary checks, delete information and take other steps required by applicable law. Parents may contact the address in Article 14. Minors must obtain the consent of a legal representative as required by law.

In the Android app, to comply with U.S. state laws, when the storefront country of the Google Play account is the United States (including when the storefront country cannot be determined), the app obtains age signals from Google Play (age range, the type of method by which that age information was established, age verification status, and the status of parental approval of significant changes to the app) after the User agrees to the Terms and this Policy. The app does not obtain age signals when the storefront country is known to be outside the United States. If the signals indicate that the User is under 13, that the User is aged 13 to 17 based solely on self-declaration (excluding age information obtained through parental supervision or age verification), that age verification is required, or that parental approval has not been given, the app displays a screen that stops use of the Service. Age signals are handled only in the device’s memory, are not stored (see the next paragraph for the record kept when the User is determined to be under 13), and are not sent to the Operator’s servers or to advertising or subscription management providers. Other information in the response from Google Play (the date of a significant change approved by a parent, and an identifier assigned to installations by supervised users) is processed on the device by the Google library built into the app; the app’s own code does not read, store or send it.

If the signals indicate that the User is under 13, the app stores on the device only the fact that the User was determined to be under 13 and the date and time of that determination, so that use of the Service remains stopped on later launches. The age range and other age-signal contents are not stored, and the app does not send this record to the Operator’s servers or to other providers. On a device with this record, later launches display the screen that stops use of the Service without obtaining age signals or checking the storefront country, and the app does not perform subscription management or ad-related processing. The app has no feature to delete this record, but it is deleted when the app’s data is cleared (uninstalling the app also deletes it from the device, but the record may return if the app is restored from an OS backup). Inquiries about a determination that appears to be incorrect are accepted at the contact in Article 14.

On launches that retrieve age signals, until the first result is available, and while the result indicates that the User is under 18, that age verification is required or that parental approval has not been given, or if retrieval fails, the app does not perform ad-related processing (consent checks, advertising SDK initialization, and ad requests and display) and does not schedule session-interruption notifications. If a later retrieval returns a result indicating none of these, ad-related processing starts from that point. However, the app cannot necessarily stop communications for measurement, diagnostics, security or similar purposes by an advertising SDK initialized earlier. Age-signal contents are never provided to advertising providers.

Users aged 13 to 17 and their parents or guardians may also make the requests described in Article 7. Age-signal information can be reviewed in Google Account settings. Questions or objections about the decision (including requests for re-review) are accepted at the contact in Article 14.

Even before an age signal is obtained, communications involving the customer ID and similar information take place after consent to initialize subscription management and determine the storefront country. After the User is determined to be under 13, the app does not communicate with the subscription management provider or send the customer ID (for later launches, see the record of an under-13 determination described above). If the incident results in, or is likely to result in, significant harm to affected individuals, or affects or is likely to affect 500 or more individuals, the Operator notifies the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) no later than 3 calendar days after the day of the assessment, and also notifies affected individuals where significant harm results or is likely
